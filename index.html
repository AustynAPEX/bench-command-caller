Setup

  1. Prop the phone side-on to the bench so the bar's full path stays in frame.
  2. Tap a plate on screen to lock tracking . Bright coloured plates track best. With black plates, put a strip of coloured tape on the collar or plate edge.
  3. Tap Arm, lie down and unrack. You'll hear "Start" once the bar is still at lockout, "Press" after it stops on your chest, and "Rack" when you lock it out.
  4. Press before the call and you get red lights and a buzzer.
